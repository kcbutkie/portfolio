Chart Glow-Up Assignment

This chart was published by "Revista Autismo" magazine's editor-in-chief in April 2025. There are many issues with this visual, such as: - The use of fidget spinners to fill in the bar charts. Aside from the potential insensitivities that arise from using fidget spinners to represent autistic individuals, the usage of discrete objects for very small percentages is misleading. - The use of different colors for the bars. The colors aren't a third dimension or representative of any additional data, so it just adds to the visual clutter.
